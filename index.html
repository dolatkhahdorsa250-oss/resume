Research & Projects

Bio-based Phase Change Materials

2025 – 2026

B.Sc. Thesis, University of Tehran

Investigated bio-based phase change materials (PCMs) for thermal energy storage. Conducted thermal and structural characterization using DSC, TGA, and FTIR, and evaluated encapsulation and vacuum impregnation methods and thermal-cycling stability. CPCMs(Composite phase change materials)based on biomass and degradable natural resources, such as delignified wood, bagasse and agricultural and industrial residues were also Invesitigated.

Bagasse-based Food Containers with Embedded PCMs

2026

School of Polymer Engineering, University of Tehran

Designed and fabricated bagasse-based food containers embedded with phase change materials to prevent heat/cold dissipation and enhance cold/heat storage. Bio-degradable CPCMs with the ability to sustain heat/cold for long periods of time and enhanced bio-degradibility were Invesitigated.
